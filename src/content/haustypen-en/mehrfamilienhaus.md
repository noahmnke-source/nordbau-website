The multi-family house is the answer to rising land prices and scarce housing: several fully-fledged apartments in one efficiently planned building. Whether for your own family plus rented units, as a capital investment or as intergenerational living – we plan it with the same precision as a single-family home, only on a larger scale.

## Building a multi-family house: economy through prefabrication

The larger the building, the stronger the advantage of prefabrication. Wall and ceiling elements for all residential units are produced in parallel in the factory, tested and dimensionally accurate, and are assembled swiftly on site. This considerably shortens the construction time compared with conventional solid construction – for a yield property, every week saved means rental income starting sooner. Thanks to scale, the price per square metre usually drops below that of a comparable single-family home.

## Sound and fire protection between the units

What distinguishes a multi-family house from a single-family home are the requirements for protection between the apartments. Our tested component build-ups in timber-frame construction meet the increased requirements for impact-sound, airborne-sound and fire protection under the applicable standards. Each residential unit is built as an Efficiency House 40 – eligible for KfW funding, and that per unit.

## Who the multi-family house is built for

The multi-family house suits owner-builders with an eye on returns, families organising several generations under one roof, and everyone who wants to make the most of scarce building land.
