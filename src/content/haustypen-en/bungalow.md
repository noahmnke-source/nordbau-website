The bungalow is the most consistent answer to a simple question: why climb stairs when everything can be on one level? Living, sleeping, bathroom and kitchen connect along short, threshold-free routes – a home that adapts to the person, not the other way round.

## Building a bungalow: one level, thoroughly considered

A good bungalow lives from its zoning. We clearly separate the public area – open-plan kitchen, dining and living room facing the garden – from the private wing with bedrooms and bathroom. A central hallway holds both together without wasting space. The large window formats bring the plot into the home: with only one level, every room sits at ground level by the garden, with direct access to the terrace.

## Why timber-frame construction suits the bungalow especially well

A bungalow has a lot of roof area and a lot of exterior wall relative to its living space – two areas where insulation determines running costs. This is exactly where timber-frame construction plays to its strengths: highly insulated, slim components, factory-made and airtight. As standard, we build every bungalow as an Efficiency House 40 with a heat pump and controlled domestic ventilation – the prerequisite for KfW funding 297/298.

## Who the bungalow is built for

The bungalow suits couples building with foresight, families who value life on one level, and everyone planning a property that will still serve in thirty years.
