The modular house takes prefabrication to its logical conclusion: instead of flat components, complete room modules are created in the factory – with floors, bathrooms, installations and finishes. On site they are assembled like precisely made building blocks. The result is a home that stands sooner, stays more predictable and grows with your requirements.

## Building a modular house: the factory delivers the finished home

The more work steps take place under controlled conditions, the more precise and weather-independent the result. With the modular house, the module leaves the hall almost finished – tested, insulated, fitted out. On the plot we join the modules within a few days; interior work is limited to the connections. For owner-builders this means the shortest route from shell to move-in, at maximum manufacturing quality.

## Planned to be extendable, not just built

A modular house does not have to do everything from the start. Two modules today, a third in five years – we plan the structural and technical connections so that later additions succeed without demolition and without compromise. This makes the modular house the economical answer for everyone who wants to start small and grow with care. Here too, we build to Efficiency House 40 standard.

## Who the modular house is built for

The modular house suits owner-builders who value pace and flexibility, and budgets that should grow with life. Anyone taking the modular idea to its smallest form will find the most compact representative in the [Tiny House](/en/haustypen/tiny-house/); the consistently cubic architecture with two fixed storeys is shown by the [Flat-Roof House](/en/haustypen/flachdachhaus/).
