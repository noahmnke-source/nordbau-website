The tiny house is not a shrunken home but a well-considered one. On 35 square metres every decision counts: where space is scarce, precision determines living quality. We build compact living with the same standard as a single-family home – fully insulated, energy-efficient and made to last for decades.

## Building a tiny house: small floor plan, great care

Small houses do not forgive weak floor plans. That is why we plan every square metre twice over: storage under the bench and the stairs, a gallery beneath the roof as a sleeping level, floor-to-ceiling glazing that visually widens the space. Kitchen and bathroom are fully-fledged, not makeshift. The result is a home that feels larger than its floor plan suggests – because nothing is superfluous.

## Fully-fledged, not provisional

A tiny house from EcoBau is neither a caravan nor a garden house. It is created in the same timber-frame construction, with the same insulation, building services and quality checks as our large houses – and reaches Efficiency House 40 standard. As a primary residence for minimalists, as a granny flat in the garden or as a high-quality office on the plot: it is small in area, but wholly there in quality.

## Who the tiny house is built for

The tiny house suits people who want to live smaller on purpose – out of conviction, for budget reasons or as a secondary use on an existing plot. Anyone looking for a little more space on one level will find it in the [Bungalow](/en/haustypen/bungalow/); anyone wishing to extend flexibly, in the [Modular House](/en/haustypen/modulhaus/).
