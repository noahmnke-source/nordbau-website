The flat-roof house is architecture without excuses: a cleanly cut building volume, two full storeys, a flat roof that brings the form to rest. No slopes, no wasted corners – every line is a decision. Anyone seeking consistent modernism in the Bauhaus spirit will find it here.

## Building a flat-roof house: full area, clear geometry

The great advantage of two full storeys under a flat roof is complete usability: no roof slope where cupboards and beds fail, full standing height right up to the exterior wall. The ground floor organises shared life as an open area onto the garden, the upper floor the retreat spaces. A void above the dining area or a roof terrace on the accessible flat roof are the most common special requests from our clients.

## The roof as a fifth facade

A flat roof is not a mere cover but usable area. As standard we prepare it for photovoltaics and, on request, build it as a green roof – this retains rainwater, adds insulation and extends the life of the seal. In timber-frame construction the highly insulated building envelope is created in the factory, airtight and dimensionally accurate, as standard to Efficiency House 40.

## Who the flat-roof house is built for

The flat-roof house suits clients who do not mistake clarity for coldness – and who know that reduction is the most demanding form of precision.
