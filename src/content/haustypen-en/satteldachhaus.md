The gable-roof house is the familiar form of the single-family home – two pitched surfaces, one ridge, one clear gable. With us this form is not copied but precisely redrawn: reduced details, large window formats, a facade that plays between render and timber. Classic in outline, modern in expression.

## Building a gable-roof house: proven and economical

The gable roof is, for good reason, the most common roof form in Germany: it sheds rain and snow safely, is low-maintenance and, with its convertible attic, creates especially affordable living space. Ground floor and attic together form a compact, well-insulated volume – little exterior surface relative to living space means low running costs. This is exactly what makes this house type the value-for-money champion among single-family homes.

## Floor plan: open below, sheltered above

The ground floor is where shared life happens – an open-plan kitchen facing the garden, plus a study, a guest WC and building services. The attic offers the more private rooms, whose sloping ceilings create a spatial character no flat roof knows: a child's room under the slope feels different from a cube. Through dormers and generous gable windows we bring light and standing height into the roof.

## Who the gable-roof house is built for

The gable-roof house suits families who want to invest their budget in area and specification rather than in elaborate geometry. Like every one of our homes, it is planned individually – from floor plan through facade to the fixture and finish selection, described in our [process](/en/ablauf/).
