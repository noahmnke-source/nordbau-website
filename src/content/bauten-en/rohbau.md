The structural shell is the work by which a construction company has to be judged. Accuracy missing here is never recovered by a finishing trade – and what is done well here, nobody ever sees. We carry out shell construction for residential buildings, offices and administration, halls and public buildings: in reinforced concrete, in masonry and in timber.

## The shell as a commission in its own right

Many clients want to award the fit-out themselves and are looking for a partner for the structure who hands over cleanly. That is exactly why the shell is a complete service with us: foundations, loadbearing walls, columns, floors, stair cores – surveyed, recorded and structurally certified. Whether we carry on from there is your decision.

## The second stage: a tight envelope

Between shell and turnkey lies the stage most often chosen in practice: the shell with a closed envelope. Roof, façade, windows and external doors are added, and the building becomes weathertight and heatable. From that point every finishing trade works independently of the weather – and the risk of construction moisture is behind you.

## Concrete, masonry, timber

What carries the load is decided by spans, fire requirements, basements and the programme, not by habit. On Maybachstraße in Stuttgart it was three basement levels in watertight construction beneath seven upper floors in reinforced concrete. In the warehouse for PVU-Group in Isernhagen, reinforced concrete columns and prestressed beams carried large column-free areas. At the day nursery in Empelde the timber frame stood on a watertight concrete slab. Three projects, three methods, the same responsibility.

## Groundworks included

The interface between earthworks and shell is where programmes most often slip. Where we carry out excavation, foundations and drainage as well, that interface disappears: one site manager, one set of records, one responsibility. At City Gate Nord in Hanover and at PVU-Group in Isernhagen we worked that way – groundworks, shell and fit-out in the same commission.
