A commercial building is not judged by how it feels to live in but by its use, its programme and its running costs. We plan and build offices and administration buildings, showrooms, practices and hospitality projects – turnkey or in clearly defined work packages.

## Designed from the operation outwards

What happens in a building every day decides its structure. A showroom needs large column-free spans and daylight deep into the plan. An administration building needs flexible layouts that can be re-divided without touching the structure. A hotel needs acoustic separation between rooms and services that stay maintainable in full operation. We settle these questions before the façade.

## Timber, modular or conventional

We are not a supplier who forces every project into the same method. Where prefabrication brings time and quality, we build in timber or in modules. Where spans, fire requirements or basements argue against it, we build conventionally – or combine both. What matters is what the building has to do.

## Also alongside an operation in use

Extensions, second phases and fit-outs in occupied buildings are part of our everyday work. That takes more than craft: coordinated delivery, clean separation, dust and noise protection and a sequence that adapts to the operation rather than the other way round.

## You set the scope

Not every client needs the same thing. We build the structural shell on its own, the shell with a closed, weathertight envelope, or the complete building with every trade through to handover. At City Gate Nord in Hanover the commission covered all three stages – groundworks, shell and fit-out from one hand.
