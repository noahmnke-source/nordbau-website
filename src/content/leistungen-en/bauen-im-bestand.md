In an existing building the construction work is rarely the hard part. The hard part is everything around it: a building in use, record drawings that do not match, and constraints that simply do not apply to a new build. We convert, extend and fit out – in housing, offices, schools and hospitals.

## Survey first, quote second

Record drawings are snapshots, often decades old. We open up critical areas, take measurements and record what is actually there. Anything that can only appear during strip-out we name as a risk – openly, rather than building it into a price that will not hold.

## Building while work continues

Dust screens, scheduled deliveries, work outside operating hours and a dedicated fire safety concept for the construction phase: that is the difference between a site beside an operation and a site that shuts the operation down. At Braunschweig hospital we fitted out six storeys while patients were treated next door.

## Work on the structure

Openings, new penetrations and transfer beams are not a demolition question but a calculation question. Every intervention gets its structural certificate before the first cut. For roof extensions we bring in our second strand: timber frame from our own plant is light enough to spare existing foundations.
