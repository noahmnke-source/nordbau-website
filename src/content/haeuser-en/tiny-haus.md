  - q: Can I put a tiny house anywhere?
    a: No. As a permanent home a tiny house is legally a building and needs a building plot, planning consent and connections for water, drainage and electricity. Which plot allows this is set by the development plan – in the open countryside it is generally not possible. We clarify that before design begins.
  - q: Is a tiny house the same as a caravan on wheels?
    a: No. Our tiny houses are fixed buildings in timber frame construction with foundations, full insulation and building services. Mobile units on a trailer chassis are subject to different rules and to limits on insulation and weight – we do not build those.
  - q: Is the effort worthwhile for so little area?
    a: Per square metre a tiny house is more expensive than a large one, because bathroom, kitchen, services and connections arise regardless of area. In total it remains considerably cheaper – and for an annexe, a garden office or a holiday unit it is often the only sensible size.

A tiny house is not a shrunken house but a considered one. On 35 square metres every decision counts: where area is scarce, precision decides the quality of living. We build compact homes to the same standard as a detached house – fully insulated, energy-efficient and made to last decades.

## A small plan, great care

Small houses do not forgive weak layouts. So we plan every square metre twice: storage under the bench and the stairs, a gallery beneath the roof as a sleeping level, full-height glazing that visually widens the space. Kitchen and bathroom are fully specified, not makeshift. The result is a house that feels larger than its plan suggests – because nothing is superfluous.

## Fully specified, not provisional

An EcoBau tiny house is neither a site hut nor a garden shed. It is built in the same timber frame construction, with the same insulation, building services and quality control as our large houses – and reaches the Efficiency House 40 standard. As a primary home for minimalists, as an annexe for the older generation or as an office in the garden.

## Individually or as a scheme

For holiday operators, municipalities and investors we produce several identical units in parallel. Because prefabrication runs in series anyway, the cost per unit falls markedly – while the standard stays identical across every house.
