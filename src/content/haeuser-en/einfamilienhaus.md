The detached house is our most frequent commission – and the one that shows most clearly what prefabrication delivers. Two roof forms shape our designs: the pitched roof as the proven, economical form with a convertible roof space, and the flat roof as a clearly cut volume with two full levels. Construction, insulation standard and manufacturing quality are identical in both.

## Pitched roof: the proven form, drawn afresh

The pitched roof is the form most commonly permitted in German development plans – and the most economical. It sheds water reliably, is structurally simple and creates living space in the roof that costs considerably less than another full storey. Modern interpretations lift it out of cliché: shallower pitches, deep overhangs, window bands reaching into the gable, façades of render and vertical timber cladding.

## Flat roof: two full storeys without slopes

The great advantage of two full storeys under a flat roof is complete usability: no sloping ceiling where wardrobes and beds fail, full headroom right up to the external wall. The ground floor organises shared life as an open area facing the garden, the upper floor the private rooms. We prepare the roof itself for photovoltaics as standard and, on request, build it as a green roof.

## Why timber frame

Wall, floor and roof elements are made in our plant under factory conditions: insulated, with windows fitted, checked and dimensionally accurate. On site the parts are joined, not built. That removes weather as a risk, keeps moisture out of the structure and delivers an airtight envelope that can be measured – the prerequisite for the Efficiency House 40 standard we build as standard.

## The design is yours

The designs on this page are inspiration, not an order catalogue. Every house is drawn for its site – for orientation, plot shape, neighbouring buildings and the question of how you actually live. Whether the design comes from our team, from your architect, or from both together is entirely open.
